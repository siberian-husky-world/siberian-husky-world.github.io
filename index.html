05 / How we work

From a Husky owner's question to a page you can use

  1. Ask what owners need

    Each guide starts from a question Husky owners keep asking in forums and groups.

  2. Find the facts

    Breed club standards, manufacturer specs and peer discussions, each linked where it is used.

  3. Make it useful

    A checker, planner or interactive tool you can try on your own dog, near the top.

  4. Check and publish

    Drafted with AI help, read by a person before it goes live, corrected when you write in.

Siberian Husky World is written with AI assistance and checked by a person before publishing. We are not a vet clinic: guides describe what owners and published sources say, and point you to a vet when home care is not enough. Read the editorial policy.
